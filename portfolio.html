About Me

Hi, I'm Alex Rivera

For over a decade I've chased light across continents — from misty mountain dawns to the electric pulse of midnight cities. Photography, for me, is less about the camera and more about presence: noticing the fleeting instant before it slips away.

My work has appeared in national galleries and independent publications, and I love collaborating with people who want images with soul.
